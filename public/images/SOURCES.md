## Indian meal image generation

Mode: built-in image generation, new image, opaque background.

Final prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial food photograph for GlucoNest website. Primary request: a beautiful authentic South Indian meal featuring soft white idli, a golden crisp dosa, a green mung-bean pesarattu, a small bowl of plain white rice, and a separate small bowl of thick white curd. Arrange all five clearly recognisable foods together on a banana leaf and simple ceramic serving dishes on a warm natural table. A couple of small bowls of coconut chutney and sambar may accompany them. Style: premium natural food photography, appetising and culturally authentic, soft daylight, earthy warm neutral colours. Composition: square image, overhead to slightly angled view, all five dishes comfortably within the central frame so the image works in a tall website crop. No people, no hands, no text, no logos, no watermarks.

## Knowledge Hub illustration

`knowledge-hub.webp` was generated using the built-in image generation tool and optimized for the Hub header.

Landscape 3:2 editorial gouache and colored-pencil illustration for an Indian type 1 diabetes learning library. An Indian young adult in a short-sleeved teal shirt with a small round white CGM on the upper arm, seated reading a tablet showing a single simple glucose trend line, with a notebook and a small steel plate with idli nearby. Warm off-white paper, muted petrol teal, ochre, terracotta, soft daylight, hand-painted textures, thoughtful and serene, beautifully simplified shapes. Frame subjects in the middle so the composition crops well. No lettering, numbers, logos or badges.
